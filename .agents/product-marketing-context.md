# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-03-10*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** AI-powered ad trafficking assistant for Google Campaign Manager 360
**What it does:** AdTraffic.ai is a conversational AI platform for CM360 ad trafficking. Its AI assistant, Kiki — the digital twin of founder Kiki Hammond — builds entire campaigns from natural language. Upload an IO, describe what you need, and Kiki creates campaigns, placements, assigns creatives, generates tags, and validates everything — with a human review step before anything goes live. 10+ years of media operations expertise encoded into an AI.
**Product name:** AdTraffic.ai (the product/company). Kiki (the AI assistant inside the product, named after founder Kiki Hammond).
**Product category:** Ad trafficking automation / CM360 automation / campaign setup and tracking / ad serving tool. Niche product — buyers may search for "CM360 automation," "ad trafficking tool," "campaign management tool," or reference the legacy bulk tool Google deprecated.
**Product type:** SaaS (B2B)
**Business model:** Tiered subscriptions — Starter ($499/mo), Professional ($1,499/mo), Enterprise (custom). No free trial. B2B clients expect to pay for pilots and shorter contract terms.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Ad trafficking in CM360 is manual, tedious, error-prone, and bottlenecks every campaign launch. For a standard IO with 50 line items, that's 2-4 hours of clicking. The person doing it is usually entry-level, undertrained, and overwhelmed.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Nothing else writes to CM360. You can read data out (CData, Windsor.ai) but nobody automates the build.
- Google deprecated their legacy bulk tool ~2.5 years ago and never replaced it.
- The bulk upload feature still exists but is clunky, manual, and hard to work with through the UI.
- Spreadsheet workarounds (export templates, fill in Excel, re-upload) are fragile and still require CM360 expertise.
**What it costs them:** The cost of a junior hire dedicated to a role with high turnover, 2-4 hours per IO of manual entry, human errors that delay launches or cause misconfigurations, constant retraining cycle.
**Emotional tension:** Campaign launches are always stressful. The trafficker is the bottleneck everyone is waiting on. One wrong CPM, one bad UTM, one missed flight date and you hear about it from the client. Nobody wants to do trafficking. It's the role people endure until they get promoted out of it.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Nothing. No one else writes to CM360 via AI or automation (post-deprecation of Google's legacy bulk tool). AdTraffic.ai is the only tool that does this.
**Secondary:** CData, Windsor.ai — read CM360 data for reporting but cannot create campaigns, placements, or tags. Google's own CM360 UI — manual but it's the status quo.
**Indirect:** Hiring more traffic coordinators. Offshore staffing. Hoping someone on the team "figures out CM360." The buying platforms (DV360, TTD, Meta) are building their own agents for their side — but nobody is building for the ad server.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Only tool that writes to CM360
- Natural language interface — no CM360 expertise required to execute
- Confirmation gates — human review before anything goes live
- Built by a trafficking veteran who's done the job for 10+ years
- Trains users as they go — the AI teaches CM360 best practices through use
**How we do it differently:** Conversational AI that understands IOs, CM360 structure, and trafficking workflows. You talk to Kiki like you'd talk to your best trafficker.
**Why that's better:** Zero human error on execution. Minutes instead of hours. The trafficker's role shifts from data entry to strategy.
**Why customers choose us:** There is no alternative. It's AdTraffic.ai or keep clicking.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Can I trust AI to write directly to my client's CM360 account?" | Confirmation gates on every action. Nothing goes live without human review and approval. Full audit trail. |
| "We have a process that works, why change?" | Works ≠ efficient. You're spending hours on what takes minutes. Your team should be driving strategy, not copy-pasting CPMs. |
| "What if it makes a mistake?" | Pre-flight validation catches errors before anything publishes. Plus human review step. That's more checks than your current manual process has. |

**Anti-persona:** Anyone not using CM360/DCM. Solo operators with one campaign a month. Teams looking for a buying platform agent (DV360, TTD, Meta) — that's not what this is.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Hours of manual clicking per IO, high trafficker turnover, long training curve, constant risk of human error on live campaigns, Google deprecated the only bulk tool and never replaced it.
**Pull:** Minutes instead of hours, zero execution errors, natural language (no CM360 expertise needed), trafficker becomes a strategic role, Kiki trains people as they use it.
**Habit:** "We've always done it this way," existing team knows the UI, fear of changing process during live campaigns, inertia of manual workflows.
**Anxiety:** Trusting AI to write to a live ad server, learning curve for the team, justifying the cost to CFO/principal, "will this actually work with our account structure?"

| Floodlight | CM360's conversion tracking tag/pixel |
| CPM | Cost per thousand impressions — the pricing model for placements |
| Flight dates | The start and end dates for a campaign or placement |
| Tags | Ad serving code snippets placed on publisher sites to serve and track ads |
| UTM | Tracking parameters appended to URLs for campaign attribution |
